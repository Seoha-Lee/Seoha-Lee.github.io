Analyzing English-German Code-Mixing in Young Women’s Speech: Token Patterns and Topic Effects

This project aims to investigate how young German women in their twenties incorporate English into everyday German conversation. We plan to conduct recorded paired discussions across a range of topics and systematically analyze the speech for English lexical items, hybrid verbs, and full English sentences. Participants will also complete surveys regarding their language choices and perceived identity, enabling an exploration of how English use reflects social meaning and topical influences in contemporary spoken discourse.
